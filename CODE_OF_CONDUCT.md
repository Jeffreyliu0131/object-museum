# Code of Conduct

Object Museum welcomes constructive participation from people with different backgrounds, identities, abilities, and levels of experience. This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and other spaces represented as part of this project.

## Expected conduct

Participants should:

- discuss ideas and code without attacking the person presenting them;
- state evidence, assumptions, and uncertainty honestly;
- give specific, actionable feedback and receive correction in good faith;
- respect consent, attribution, and conflicting accounts;
- protect the privacy of contributors and people mentioned in examples; and
- help keep the project accessible, safe, and useful within its stated scope.

## Unacceptable conduct

The following behavior is not accepted:

- harassment, threats, stalking, intimidation, or discriminatory abuse;
- sexualized attention or imagery directed at another participant;
- sustained disruption, trolling, or knowingly misleading claims;
- publishing another person's private or identifying information without permission;
- submitting real family, personal, confidential, or identifying material to this public project; or
- retaliation against someone who reports a concern or participates in its review.

Disagreement, strong technical criticism, and requests for better evidence are allowed when they address the work and remain respectful.

## Privacy boundary

Do not include real names, faces, voices, family stories, contact details, precise locations, credentials, private documents, browser databases, or unsanitized logs in a conduct report or public thread. Use a private maintainer channel and share only the minimum information needed to evaluate the concern.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through a contact method listed on a repository maintainer's GitHub profile. If no private method is available, use the [private-contact request form](https://github.com/Jeffreyliu0131/object-museum/issues/new?template=private_contact.yml). That request is public: ask only for a private route and include no incident, affected-person, or personal-data details.

Maintainers will review reports in good faith, limit access to the information, and consider context, severity, impact, prior behavior, and the safety of affected people. Possible responses include clarification, content removal, a private warning, interaction limits, temporary suspension, or permanent exclusion from project spaces.

Maintainers may remove contributions or comments that violate this policy. They will avoid public disclosure of report details unless disclosure is necessary for safety or required by law.

Security vulnerabilities follow the private process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
